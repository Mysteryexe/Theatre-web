Opened in 1950, King’s Theatre brings first-class motion-picture entertainment to Ramsgate in modern and comfortable surroundings. With excellent presentation and a welcoming atmosphere, it proudly invites audiences in 1951 to enjoy the finest films and make King’s Theatre their regular cinema.
